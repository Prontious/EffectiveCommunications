# 6. The Dedication of a Church

Between the Proper of the Season and the saints' days the book prints the office for the
anniversary of the **Dedication of a Church** (*In dedicatione ecclesie*), a double. Its
texts celebrate the church building as an image of the heavenly Jerusalem — a theme with
special resonance for an Order founded beside the Holy Sepulchre.

---

## Lauds of the Dedication (scan p. 519)

**Latin**

> *[End of Matins:] Psalmus* Te Deum. ℣. *sacerdotalis.* Hec est domus Domini firmiter
> edificata, alleluia. ℟. Bene fundata est supra firmam petram, alleluia.
>
> In laudibus. *Antiphona.* Domum tuam, Domine, decet sanctitudo in longitudinem dierum.
> *Psalmus* Dominus regnavit.
>
> *Antiphona.* Domus mea domus orationis vocabitur. *Psalmus* Iubilate.
>
> *Antiphona.* Hec est domus Domini firmiter edificata; bene fundata est supra firmam petram.
> *Psalmus* Deus deus.
>
> *Antiphona.* Bene fundata est domus Domini supra firmam petram. *Psalmus* Benedicite.
>
> *Antiphona.* Lapides preciosi omnes muri tui, et turres Hierusalem gemmis edificabuntur.
> *Psalmus* Laudate.
>
> *Capitulum.* Vidi civitatem sanctam Hierusalem novam descendentem de celo a Deo, paratam
> sicut sponsam ornatam viro suo.
>
> *Hymnus* Hoc in templo *[summe Deus]*. ℣. Adorate Dominum, alleluia. ℟. In aula sancta
> eius *[, alleluia]*.
>
> *Ad* Benedictus *antiphona.* Mane surgens Iacob erigebat lapidem in titulum, fundens oleum
> desuper; votum vovit Domino: Vere locus iste sanctus est, et ego nesciebam.
>
> *Oratio.* Deus, qui invisibiliter omnia contines, et tamen pro salute generis humani signa
> tue potentie visibiliter ostendis: templum hoc potentie tue habitatione illustra, ut omnes
> qui huc deprecaturi conveniunt, ex quacunque tribulatione ad te clamaverint, consolationis
> tue beneficia consequantur. Per Dominum.

**Translation**

> *[End of Matins:]* The *Te Deum*. *The priest's versicle:* This is the house of the Lord,
> firmly built, alleluia. ℟. It is well founded upon a firm rock, alleluia.
>
> **At Lauds.** *Antiphon:* Holiness becomes thy house, O Lord, unto length of days.
> (Ps 92:5) *Psalm 92* The Lord hath reigned.
>
> *Antiphon:* My house shall be called a house of prayer. (Is 56:7; Mt 21:13) *Psalm 99*
> Shout with joy.
>
> *Antiphon:* This is the house of the Lord, firmly built; it is well founded upon a firm
> rock. (cf. Mt 7:25) *Psalm 62* O God, my God.
>
> *Antiphon:* The house of the Lord is well founded upon a firm rock. *Canticle* Bless [the
> Lord].
>
> *Antiphon:* All thy walls are precious stones, and the towers of Jerusalem shall be built
> with gems. (cf. Tob 13:21) *Psalm 148* Praise.
>
> *Chapter:* I saw the holy city, the new Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God,
> prepared as a bride adorned for her husband. (Rev 21:2)
>
> *Hymn* In this temple [, O God most high] — *the second part of the Dedication hymn* Urbs
> beata Hierusalem, *printed at first Vespers (scan p. 516)*. ℣. Adore the Lord, alleluia.
> ℟. In his holy court [, alleluia].
>
> *Antiphon at the Benedictus:* Jacob, rising in the morning, set up a stone for a monument,
> pouring oil upon it; he made a vow to the Lord: Truly this place is holy, and I knew it
> not. (cf. Gen 28:16–18)
>
> *Collect:* O God, who invisibly containest all things, and yet for the salvation of mankind
> showest visibly the signs of thy power: fill this temple with the indwelling of thy power,
> that all who gather here to pray, from whatever trouble they cry to thee, may obtain the
> benefits of thy consolation. Through [our] Lord.

### The hymn *Hoc in templo* (printed at first Vespers, scan p. 516)

> Hoc in templo, summe Deus, / exoratus adveni, / et clementi bonitate / precum vota
> suscipe; / largam benedictionem / hic infunde iugiter.
> Hic promereantur omnes / petita accipere, / et adepta possidere / cum sanctis perenniter, /
> paradisum introire, / translati in requiem.
> Gloria et honor Deo …

> In this temple, O God most high, come when we pray to thee, and in thy gracious goodness
> receive the desires of our prayers; pour out here continually thy abundant blessing.
> Here may all be worthy to receive what they ask, and to keep for ever with the saints what
> they have gained, and, carried to their rest, to enter paradise.
> Glory and honour to God …
