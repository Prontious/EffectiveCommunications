# 3. The psalter — weekday Lauds

On weekdays (*ferie*) Lauds follows the same pattern throughout the psalter:

1. **Psalm 50** *Miserere mei Deus*;
2. a psalm proper to the day;
3. **Psalm 62** *Deus deus meus, ad te de luce vigilo* [with Ps 66];
4. an **Old Testament canticle** proper to the day, printed in full;
5. **Psalm 148** *Laudate Dominum de celis* [with Pss 149–150];

then the chapter, hymn, versicle, and the *Benedictus* with its antiphon. The preces,
collect and suffrages follow as laid down in the rubrics (see [section 1](01-rubrics.md)).
Psalm texts are cued by their opening words only, as in the book; the canticles, chapter
and hymn are printed in full and given in full here.

On weekdays the chapter is always *Fratres, vigilate* (1 Cor 16:13–14) and the versicle
*In matutinis, Domine, meditabor in te*. On Sunday the psalms are 92, 99, 62, the
*Benedicite* and 148; the Sunday chapter is *Benedictio et claritas* (Rev 7:12).
